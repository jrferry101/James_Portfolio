A Passion Project: AI and VR for Home Buyers

​In my personal life I'm constantly thinking about how I can use AI to make my experiences better. The wonderful thing about AI is that it is moving FAST, this also means that by the time someone is reading this, it's more than likely that this idea has already come to light and a successful business has been made out of it. 

While searching for a new home for my family, I realized how limited and time-consuming the traditional home-buying experience still is. That sparked an idea: what if we could bring model homes to buyers—virtually? I'm developing an XR application that allows users to explore homes through immersive 360° experiences using VR headsets. The technology already exists for capturing real-world spaces, but I’m focused on expanding it to let buyers visualize homes directly from blueprints. Imagine being able to "walk through" a home before it's built—and eventually customize finishes, layouts, and features in real time.
