A Little About
My Product Management Journey.

Product management has been in my sights since I was young. I shadowed my father in his software engineering role in 2014 and I learned firsthand what product managers do as I observed their sprint planning.

Ever since that day I have set my sights on becoming a competitive product manager. I learned everything I could from experienced product managers and directors by shadowing them. I made lasting connections and learned the habits of a good product manager.

​I hope you find the value in my current efforts as you see the positive feedback former coworkers have had to say about my product management efforts and by observing the quality of projects I have initiated.
